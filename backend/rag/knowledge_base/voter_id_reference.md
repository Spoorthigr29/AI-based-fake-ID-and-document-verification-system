# Voter ID (EPIC) Official Verification Knowledge Reference

**Document Type:** VOTER_ID
**Issuing Authority:** Election Commission of India (ECI)
**Statutory Framework:** Representation of the People Act, 1950 & 1951

---

## 1. Expected Document Fields and Structure

An authentic Elector's Photo Identity Card (EPIC) contains:
1. **EPIC Number:**
   - 10-character alphanumeric string: 3 uppercase English letters (Functional Area / Assembly constituency code) followed by 7 numeric digits (e.g., `ABC1234567` or `XYZ7654321`).
2. **Elector Demographics:**
   - Elector's Full Name, Father's / Husband's Name, Gender (`MALE` / `FEMALE`), Date of Birth or Age on qualifying date.
3. **Assembly & Polling Details:**
   - Assembly Constituency Number & Name, Part Number & Name, Polling Station location.
4. **Security Features:**
   - ECI Hologram seal, barcode / QR code on newer e-EPIC variants, security text border.

---

## 2. Common Inconsistencies & Verification Guidelines

1. **Older Paper/Laminated EPIC vs. e-EPIC:**
   - Older black-and-white or laminated cards may feature lower print resolution or lack QR codes. This is normal for historic issuances and should not trigger fraud flags.
   - Newer color PVC and e-EPIC cards feature high-resolution photographs and secure digital QR codes.
