# Indian Passport Official Verification Knowledge Reference

**Document Type:** PASSPORT
**Issuing Authority:** Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Consular, Passport and Visa (CPV) Division, Government of India
**Statutory Framework:** Passports Act, 1967 & ICAO Document 9303 (Machine Readable Travel Documents)

---

## 1. Expected Document Fields and Structure

An authentic Indian Passport biodata page contains:
1. **Passport Number:**
   - Starts with one uppercase letter (e.g., `A`, `B`, `J`, `K`, `L`, `M`, `P`, `R`, `S`, `T`, `U`, `V`, `Z`) followed by 7 digits (e.g., `A1234567`).
2. **Document Type & Country Code:**
   - Type `P` (Ordinary Passport), Country Code `IND`.
3. **Cardholder Demographics:**
   - Given Names, Surname, Nationality (`INDIAN`), Sex (`M`, `F`, `X`), Date of Birth (`DD/MM/YYYY`), Place of Birth.
4. **Dates & Issuing Authority:**
   - Date of Issue, Date of Expiry (standard 10-year validity for adults, 5-year for minors), Place of Issue.
5. **Machine Readable Zone (MRZ):**
   - 2 lines of 44 alphanumeric characters each adhering to ICAO 9303 Type 3 standard.
   - Contains passport number, check digits, birth date, expiry date, and holder name.

---

## 2. Official Verification Procedures

1. **MRZ Format & Mathematical Checksum Verification:**
   - Calculate and verify check digits for passport number, date of birth, date of expiry, and composite checksum.
2. **Visual & MRZ Cross-Consistency:**
   - Compare name, passport number, DOB, sex, and expiry between the upper visual inspection zone (VIZ) and the lower MRZ.
3. **Security Features & Biometrics:**
   - Ghost photograph, optical security guilloche patterns, intaglio printing texture.
