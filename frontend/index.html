VERIFICATION MODULES

Multi-Modal AI Screening Architecture

Six synchronized forensic engines analyze every uploaded identity document across optical, structural, and cryptographic dimensions.

Optical Quality & Glare Analyzer

Evaluates Laplacian blur variance, specular reflection, exposure histograms, and lighting uniformity to prevent fraudulent unreadable scans.

Multi-Engine OCR & Layout

Deep neural text recognition engine extracts Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Document Numbers, and Address with character-level confidence scores.

Biometric Facial Verification

Performs high-precision facial landmark localization, alignment, cosine similarity calculation, and passive 3D liveness detection against applicant selfies.

Forensic ELA & Tamper Detection

Error Level Analysis (ELA) and copy-move forensic algorithms detect digital modifications, font splices, replaced photos, and altered numerical digits.

QR Code & Digest Cross-Check

Decodes encoded 2D barcodes (UIDAI Secure QR, Parivahan QR, ICAO MRZ) and cross-validates visual OCR fields against embedded encrypted metadata.

Multi-Modal Risk Engine & RAG

Aggregates 24 quantitative risk signals into a deterministic score (0–100) and uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide clear human explanations.

ENTERPRISE SECURITY

Institutional Grade Data Protection

VerifyX AI is engineered with privacy-first standards to safeguard identity assets:

  • In-Memory Processing: Documents processed without permanent disk caching.
  • PII Masking: Aadhaar numbers masked (XXXX XXXX 1234) across all audit logs.
  • Encrypted Transmission: End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption across frontend and backend.
Audit Trail & Governance

Every verification event is assigned an immutable SHA-256 digest and timestamp for regulatory compliance.
